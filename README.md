plumber misses a call, AI texts the caller back and books the job. Day one:
list 100 plumbers on Google Maps and send each a demo video. Twilio costs
$15. Close two sales in 30 days and that is $498; most likely $250–500.
No marketplace queue, no reviews needed — it sells recovered revenue,
not a promise.
